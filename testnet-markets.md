# Why testnet coin markets exist

Test networks exist so developers can build and break things without risking real money. The catch is getting enough test coins.

## The faucet problem

Faucets give out small amounts, often with captchas, waits and daily limits. That works for a first transaction, but not for load tests, Lightning channels, exchange integrations or CI pipelines that need a steady supply.

## What a market adds

On AltQuick you can buy Bitcoin signet, testnet4 and testnet3 coins for a small amount of Bitcoin, and they withdraw instantly to any testnet wallet, with no faucet drip limits. When a project is done, the leftover coins can be sold back, so they go to the next developer who needs them instead of sitting idle.

The price of test coins stays tiny, but it gives people a reason to return coins to circulation, which faucets on their own can't do.
