# AltQuick press kit

Logos, key facts and monthly numbers for writing about AltQuick.

## Key facts

- AltQuick is a US-based exchange founded in 2015 that runs its own exchange engine.
- It lists Bitcoin signet, testnet4 and testnet3 markets alongside coin markets, all traded against Bitcoin.
- The trading fee is 1%. AltQuick uses no fiat currency.
- It offers a public API, AltQuick Swap for quick market orders, and a testnet faucet.
- Its affiliate program shares 50% of trading fees with partners, and referred accounts stay credited for life.
- Contact: [hello@altquick.com](mailto:hello@altquick.com)
