How to buy Curecoin (CURE) with Bitcoin

Curecoin (CURE) trades against Bitcoin on AltQuick with a public order book and API.

How to buy Curecoin with Bitcoin

  1. Create a free account on altquick.com and deposit Bitcoin.
  2. Open the CURE/BTC market, check the order book, and place a limit or market order.
  3. Withdraw CURE to your own wallet, or keep it on AltQuick to trade later.

To sell, deposit CURE and place a sell order on the same market to receive Bitcoin.
