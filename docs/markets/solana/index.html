How to buy Solana (SOL) with Bitcoin

Solana (SOL) trades against Bitcoin on AltQuick with a public order book and API.

How to buy Solana with Bitcoin

  1. Create a free account on altquick.com and deposit Bitcoin.
  2. Open the SOL/BTC market, check the order book, and place a limit or market order.
  3. Withdraw SOL to your own wallet, or keep it on AltQuick to trade later.

To sell, deposit SOL and place a sell order on the same market to receive Bitcoin.
